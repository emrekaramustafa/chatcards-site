Tek paragraf

ChatCards, WhatsApp’tan dışa aktarılan grup ya da iki kişilik sohbeti telefonda analiz eden ve paylaşılabilir “ödül” kartları üreten bir iPhone ve Android uygulamasıdır. “Grubun Gevezesi”, “Gece Kuşu”, “Bekle ki Cevap Versin” ve Türk gruplarına özgü “IBAN Dedektifi” gibi 20’den fazla ödül, iki kişilik sohbette ise yedi ölçüte dayalı “sohbeti kim taşıyor” hükmü çıkar. Analiz tamamen cihazda yapılır; mesajlar, isimler ve sohbet istatistikleri gönderilmez, sohbet yapay zekâya yüklenmez. İlk analiz ücretsizdir.

One paragraph

ChatCards is an iPhone and Android app that analyses a WhatsApp group or one-on-one chat export on the phone and turns it into shareable award cards. Groups get 20+ awards such as The Yapper, Night Owl and Snail Mail; one-on-one chats get a seven-measure “who carries the conversation” verdict. The analysis runs entirely on the device: messages, names and chat statistics are never sent, and the chat is never uploaded to an AI. The first analysis is free.

What it doesn’t do: No AI · No chat upload · No account · No in-app ads · No read-receipt claims

Privacy fact card

  • Stays on the device: the chat file, message text, participant names, phone numbers, analysis results and cards.
  • Leaves the device (no content): anonymous usage events — screens and funnel steps, Mixpanel, EU data region; purchase verification — RevenueCat; on Android, store attribution (Play Install Referrer campaign codes); on iPhone, Apple’s Ads attribution token (via RevenueCat; no advertising ID, no ATT); a request for a public settings file (config.json, no identifiers). Crash reporting (Sentry, EU, sending of personal data off) is built in but currently switched off.
  • Never in any event: message or participant counts (not even as ranges), relationship type, chat or group name, person names, file names, phone numbers.
  • Complete list of Mixpanel events: App First Open, Open Page, Import Started, Analysis Completed, Analysis Failed, Share Started, Share Result, Free Unlock Consumed, Paywall Viewed, Purchase Result, Review Prompted. Properties are values from fixed lists, card counts and yes/no flags only. Every event also carries the app version, platform, interface language and install source.
  • Sent to RevenueCat: an anonymous app user ID, product ID, purchase token or transaction information, store, and for earlier subscriptions the subscription status and expiry; Mixpanel’s anonymous installation ID (so purchase events can be measured under the same anonymous ID).

Details and a field-by-field description: Privacy policy.

Press FAQ

  • Does it break WhatsApp’s rules? No. It reads the file the user creates with WhatsApp’s own “Export chat”; it doesn’t access the WhatsApp service or API.
  • Why one-time? People analyse a few chats a year, so a subscription doesn’t fit. You pay once and nothing renews, so there’s no forgotten subscription to worry about.
  • Why iPhone only, no iPad? The cards are designed for phone screens and Story size; the first versions focus on the phone.
  • Any relationship with Meta? None. ChatCards is independent; the WhatsApp logo is not used.
