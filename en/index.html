Ready in 3 steps

No chat upload to a server. No messages sent to an AI service. The analysis is computed from the export file on your phone.

1

Open the chat in WhatsApp

A group or a one-on-one chat.

2

Export chat → Without media

Android: ⋮ → More → Export chat. iPhone: tap the chat name at the top → Export Chat.

3

Pick ChatCards
